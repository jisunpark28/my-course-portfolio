1. [STEP 1: Pick a Topic]
   Enter ANY topic you like (e.g., "Harry Potter", "Cooking", "BTS").
   The AI will generate a secret word based on your topic.

2. [STEP 2: Use the Hint]
   The AI also provides a helpful HINT describing the word.
   Read the hint carefully to guess the right word!

3. [STEP 3: Guess Letters]
   Guess the word letter by letter (A-Z).
   If you guess wrong, the man gets closer to being hanged.

4. [Rules]
   - You have 6 Chances (Head, Body, Arms, Legs).
   - If the drawing is complete, you lose.

5. [Replay]
   After the game ends, you can choose to play again with the
   SAME topic to get a new word instantly!
