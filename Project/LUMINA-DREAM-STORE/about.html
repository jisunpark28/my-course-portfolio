Welcome to Lumina Dream Store

A complex system that works is invariably found to have evolved from a simple system that worked.

Meet the Developer: Jisun Park

I am a programming student at Seneca College and, importantly, a mother. My daughter is brave and joyful, yet she carries many fears within her much like I did when I was her age. Even with the warmest care from my parents, overcoming my own anxieties was a different challenge altogether.

Looking back, the courage that allowed me to speak in front of others and compete came from unexpected places: stories in books, the encouraging smiles of strangers, and heartfelt connections with friends. These "objective" sparks of interest from the world often gave me the bravest push.

The Heart Behind the Project

Currently, my daughter is struggling with a fear of swimming. Should she stop because she is afraid? I want her to discover the pure joy and sense of achievement that comes from not giving up. While I may not know each of you personally, I know that everyone has precious moments of "first successes" and overcome challenges.

Will you share your courage with our children's dreams and fears?
Will you share the stories of the dreams you once reached for?

This is why I created Lumina Dream Store. It is a space where a child's first grocery trip can inspire a senior's first kiosk payment, creating a beautiful cycle of intergenerational courage.
